# Manual issue search (pre-drawn issues, method per prereg/CHANGES.md 2026-10-21)

20 issues (2 per ordinary window, drawn by seed before reading), 2,775 articles in total.
v2 triage was run on every article with no keyword filter. Claude read every model-accepted article and every rejected
article containing broad sky vocabulary (29).

| | Count |
|---|---|
| Real sky observations found | **1** (bright meteor at Newport, N.Y., Feb 1851, letter to the Tribune) |
| Caught by the keyword filter | 1 of 1 |
| Accepted by the model | 1 of 1 |
| Rejected articles with sky vocabulary that were real reports | 0 of 29 (expressions, explosions, ships named Comet, town of Aurora) |

**Reading:** consistent with high recall, but with one event in the sample this cannot estimate recall: 1/1 has a 95% interval
of 21-100%. What it does show is how rare sky reports are in an ordinary issue, about 1 per 2,800 articles. It also shows
that the vocabulary screen turns up no hidden reports among the rejections. Not covered: articles with no sky
vocabulary at all, which were not read.
