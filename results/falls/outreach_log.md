# Institutional inquiries and research outcomes

Updated 12 November 2026. This is a project-maintained summary of inquiries, public sources and research outcomes, attributed to institutions. It is not a transcript of correspondence or a statement issued by those institutions. Dates below refer to the project's recorded inquiries and updates.

An unresolved inquiry is not evidence that a reported stone was a meteorite. Likewise, a records search finding nothing does not prove a stone never existed or was never received.

## Research conducted in public records

- **Smithsonian Institution Archives, 2026-11-05:** the project examined selected folders in RU 7177, the George P. Merrill Papers, including the meteorite correspondence index and relevant alphabetical correspondence. No matching record for the three Smithsonian inquiries above was found in those folders. The search was limited to the material examined.
- **Printed USNM accession lists, 2026-11-10:** the project checked annual reports for fiscal years ending in 1899, 1900, 1903, 1904, 1908, 1917 and 1918. No matching accession was found for those three cases. Examined-and-returned material need not appear in accession lists.
- **Carrington newspapers, 2026-11-09:** research in the [Carrington City Library digital archive](https://carringtonlibrary.advantage-preservation.com/) traced the 1910 stone through the *Weekly Independent*, including 27 January and 15 December 1910. It also uncovered a separate Melville stone reportedly donated to Rainbow Gardens in 1933; that is a research lead, not an established meteorite.
- **Bigelow:** the *Globe*, 7 June 1937, pp. 1 and 6, describes the museum inquiry; 14 June 1937, pp. 1 and 5, reports a possible October 1934 fireball. The temporal association is unproven.
- **Mathwig / Fairmont:** the *Martin County Independent*, 15 February 1911, p. 1, and 8 March 1911, p. 1, document the displayed object and a contemporary suggestion that it was related to Estherville. Neither supplies a chemical analysis.

Historical names above identify people in the source records. This summary omits present-day staff contact details, correspondence quotations, internal deliberations and institution-supplied attachments.
