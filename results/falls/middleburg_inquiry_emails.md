## 1. Jacksonville Historical Society
**Subject:** Question about a meteorite shown at the 1888 Sub-Tropical Exposition

Hi there!

My name is Tyler Collo, and I'm a computer science student at The George Washington University. As a side project I'm
researching 19th-century meteorite reports in digitized newspapers, and I came across one tied to the Sub-Tropical
Exposition.

In January 1888 the *Palatka Daily News* reported that a stone of about 200 pounds, thought to be a meteorite, was
found near Middleburg in Clay County. The finder, A. S. Chalker, gave it to Messrs. Sweeny and Allen of the steamer
*Twilight* to take to the Exposition in Jacksonville. Other papers said samples were sent to the astronomer Richard
Proctor. Here's the article: https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn89070049/1888-01-07/ed-1/?sp=4

I can't find this stone in the scientific meteorite catalogs, so I'm hoping you can tell me:
1. Do you have any Exposition catalogues, exhibitor lists or records that mention a meteorite or "meteoric stone" from
   Middleburg?
2. Is anything known about where the Exposition's mineral exhibits went after it closed in 1888?

Even a pointer to the right collection would be a big help.

Best,
Tyler
