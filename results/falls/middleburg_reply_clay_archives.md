the *Twilight*: https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn89070049/1888-01-07/ed-1/?sp=4. I'm happy to send the full list.

Budington family's land around Middleburg, could that be a misprint for a W. G. Budington? If your records show a
W. G. Budington around 1888, that would help pin down where it fell.
