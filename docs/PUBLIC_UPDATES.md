## Middleburg and the Sub-Tropical Exposition
University of Florida Special & Area Studies Collections supplied an exhibition-catalogue check: the Clay County section lists “Sample Iron Rock, from Middleburg”. The connection to the stone described in newspapers is a project inference, not a unique specimen identification. Supplied scans are not republished. The object's later whereabouts remain unknown.

The independently public scientific verdict is G. F. Kunz, Transactions of the New York Academy of Sciences 7:135 (February 1888): https://archive.org/details/transactionsofne78788newy/page/135/mode/1up . Kunz identifies the supposed Middleburg meteorite at the exposition as limonite.
