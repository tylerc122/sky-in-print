Source: NASA GSFC Five Millennium Canon catalogs (Espenak & Meeus), pages SE/LE 1701-1800, 1801-1900 and
1901-2000 from https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/, retrieved 2026-10-20. Parsed by
`src/skyinprint/parse_eclipses.py` into `eclipses_1701_2000.csv` (date and time of greatest eclipse in
Terrestrial Dynamical Time, kind, and type code: T total, A annular, H hybrid, P partial, N penumbral).
Visibility from the US is not encoded. The pilot uses dates only (a date-accuracy control).
