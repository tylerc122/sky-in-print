Notes:

General:

GNIS: Geographic Names Information System, U.S. Geological Survey.

Ohio: 1924: Notes the publication of a book by William H. 
Alexander, "Climatological History of Ohio," published by the 
Engineering Experiment Station, O.S.U., Columbus.


Locations: 

Alabama: There are two Madisons. Station in Climatological Data is 
in Madison County, 34.42, -86.45.

Colorado: Le Roy (near) is in Logan County; P.O. Fleming (1925). 
North Lake, 7/7/28: No county given. There are several features 
listen in GNIS: a lake, 40.43, -106.41, and trail, 40.45, -106.43, 
both in Routt County; a dam and reservoir, same coordinates, 
37.14, -105.02, in Las Animas County. Because of wide differences, 
ambiguities since dam sites often used, but users of lake and 
trail may have been source, no coordinates are listed in 
tabulation. Rugh Ranch, Laramie County, P.O.: Logcabin: GNIS does 
not have listing for Logcabin, so coordinates for County, from 
Getty, 40.40, -105.28, have been used.

District of Columbia: (1926:) Washington: Weather Bureau Building, 
24th and M Streets.

Maryland: Ferry Landing, Calvert County. P.O.: Owings;            
Pleasant Hill, Baltimore County. P.O.: Owings Mills. (1926:) 
Fallston, Harford County, P.O.: Bagley.

Michigan: Ruse, Marquette County: no listing found, so county 
coordinates, 46.32, -87.38, have been used. Sack Bay has been 
taken as Sac Bay.

Minnesota: Minnibigoshish, Itasca County, RFD 41375, Deer River = 
coordinates 47.20, -93.48, Itasca County. Coordinates have been 
taken, however, as those of Winnibigoshish Dam, Cass County, 
47.26, -93.03. Big Falls is in Koochiching County (1930). Pigeon 
River Bridge, Cook County, Mineral Center (1925).

Montana: Barthelmess Ranch, Southern Custer County; Goldbutte, 
Toole County; Mineota, Hill County, Rudyard; Sentinel Butte Pass, 
Garfield County, Phon; Sioux Pass (near), Richland County;  Upper 
Yaak River, Lincoln County, Yaak (1925). Dry Land Experiment 
Station, Jefferson County, P.O.: Cardwell; Barthelmess Ranch: 
Custer County, P.O.: Coalwood; Sarpy Creek: Treasure County, P.O.: 
Hysham. (April 1926). Cutbank, 8/14/29, taken as Cut Bank, as 
listed in GNIS. Phillipsburg, 7/22/27, taken as typo for 
Philipsburg. Sioux City, July, 1928, taken as error for Sioux 
Pass. "Sioux City" occurs only in the July 1928 listing, but there 
is no such place in the list of stations, and Sioux Pass, which is 
listed, has been in tabulation of auroras for considerable time 
both before and after this date. Sun River Canyon, Teton County, 
2/27/29: Coordinates taken as those of Sun River Pass, GNIS, 
47.57, -112.59. White River, 9/13/28: No White River is listed, 
taken as typo for White Water, which has numerous auroral 
notations.  

	During this period, 1924-1932, Lewiston occurs only once, on 
10/20/32. But Lewiston is not included in the list of stations. 
GNIS does list a Lewiston in Montana, Jefferson County, 46.28 N, 
111.59 W, but it is listed as historical. Lewiston has therefore 
been taken as a typo for Lewistown, 47.04 N, 109.25 W,  which is 
included in the list of stations.

North Dakota: Dogden Butte taken as Butte in GNIS, which gives the 
history of the name as originally Dogden for Dog Den Buttes, but 
changed to Butte by John Scroth, Postmaster in 1927. 
Climatological Data combines the two names and lists it as Dogden 
Butte. Metigoshi, 12/18/27, taken as typo for Metigoshe. Powers 
Lake, Burke County (1925). Stowers, Adams County, P.O.: Lemmon, 
coordinates 46.07, -102.56. But coordinates taken as those of 
Stowers Cemetery, 46.03, -102.01 (GNIS) (1928). Oaks, occurring 
only once, on 5/16/30, has been assumed to be a typo for Oakes, a 
frequent source of auroral notations. Carlson, occurring only in 
March 1930, is not included in the list of stations, and has been 
taken as a typo for Carson, which is well represented in the list 
of auroras.

South Dakota: Bellefourche, Butte County: coordinates taken as 
those of Belle Fourche, 44.40, -103.51, GNIS. Cedar View, 
Washabaugh County, taken as same as Cedarview. P.O given as 
Kodaka. GNIS does not give coordinates for either Kodaka or 
Washabaugh County. Getty lists Washabaugh County, with a GNIS 
I.D., but without coordinates. 

Utah: Hole-in-the-Rock Ranger Station, 10/17/28: no county is 
given, or other identifying information. Not in GNIS. Maybe the 
historical section of the National Park Service can identify, but 
have not tried this.

Virginia: Massinacac, Buckingham County, P.O.: Guinea Mills 
(1925).

Washington: Gunn's Ranch, 1929: Climatological Data gives no 
county, but does include in Methow drainage basin. GNIS gives 
several features under Methow, all in Okanogan County - 
coordinates have been taken as those of Methow Valley, 48.39, -
120.32, a former watercourse is at 48.02, -119.53, and an 
inhabited place at 48.07, -120.00. Stockdill's Ranch, Okanogan 
County, 1929: coordinates taken as those of the county, 48.33, 
-119.44.

Wyoming: Clarkelen, Campbell County: no listing found, coordinates 
taken as those of the county, 44.15, -105.33, Getty.  Middle Fork 
(near), Fremont County; Quaking Aspen Creek, Park County; 
Rockypoint now (1925) one word, not two. Pathfinder, Natrona 
County: coordinates taken as those for Pathfinder Dam, 42.28, -
106.51, GNIS. Ervay, Natrona County, coordinates have been taken 
as those of Ervay Basin, 42.55 N, 107.26 W.
