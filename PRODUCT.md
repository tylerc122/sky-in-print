# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Curious members of the general public exploring a museum-like science exhibit, with no specialist knowledge required. The approved docs/PUBLIC_SITE_BRIEF.md supplies the audience, tone, evidence rules and accessibility requirements.

## Product Purpose
Sky In Print follows American newspaper reports of fireballs and alleged meteorite falls to their documented endings. Visitors explore a map, read a source chain, and distinguish recognized meteorites, terrestrial objects, hoaxes and open investigations. Build a portable static site in site/ with an explicit data release.

## Brand Personality
Curious, precise, warm. Visually confident and occasionally spectacular, as requested by the user. Let a reconstruction and actual evidence create wonder.

## Anti-references
No discovery hype, specialist-only dashboards, misleading meteor-frequency charts or unsupported scientific precision. No account flows, live feeds or decorative statistics.

## Design Principles
- Lead with exploration and make source evidence one click away.
- Follow a report through its correction, not just its dramatic first appearance.
- Separate a publishing location from an observation or fall location.
- Make uncertainty and archive coverage visible.
- Use spectacle to explain the reconstruction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable fonts, keyboard-accessible atlas with equivalent list navigation, mobile layout with 16px minimum gutters, light and dark themes, reduced-motion support and labels alongside status colors.
