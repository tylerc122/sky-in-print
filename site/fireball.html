The fireball of December 21, 1876

Seven towns reported the fireball overhead. The track fitted to them passes 17 km from the Rochester, Indiana meteorite, which was not used in the fit.

How the track was fitted

Illustrative motion only. Speed, duration and altitude are not reconstructed.
